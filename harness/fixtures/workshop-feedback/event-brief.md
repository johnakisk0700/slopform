# Synthetic smartphone photography workshop

This is an authored fictional event, not a real campaign or evidence that the
application's bot supports this interview. The machine-readable event truth is
[event-brief.json](event-brief.json). It accompanies the
[showcase scenario plan](../../../docs/showcase-scenarios.md); the existing runtime
V2 questionnaire remains a separate contract.

«Φωτογραφίζω με το κινητό» takes place on **12 September 2026, 14:00–18:00,
Europe/Athens**, at the invented «Εργαστήρι Ακτίδα» in Thessaloniki. One hundred
adults use their own smartphones. Ten groups of ten, G01–G10, practice with a lead
instructor and three tutors. There are no real people, contacts or addresses.

The main hall has a projector, indoor worktables and chairs. A courtyard provides
daylight practice; a covered side gallery provides shelter. Demonstration seats
have independent front/center/rear and left/center/right positions. A rear-right
seat can experience both sound and screen problems; most seats need not.

| Time        | Activity                                                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 14:00–14:15 | Check-in and group assignment                                |
| 14:15–15:00 | Focus, exposure adjustment and framing demonstration         |
| 15:00–15:35 | G01–G05 courtyard light exercise; G06–G10 indoor composition |
| 15:35–15:45 | Groups change spaces                                         |
| 15:45–16:20 | Groups take the other exercise                               |
| 16:20–16:35 | Break                                                        |
| 16:35–17:10 | Basic editing with the phone's built-in editor               |
| 17:10–17:45 | Voluntary image critique; twelve images discussed            |
| 17:45–18:00 | Recap and next-day handout announcement                      |
| 18:30       | Fictional feedback invitation                                |

The light exercise compares the same tabletop object in direct and shaded light,
with an exposure adjustment. Composition uses three frames of a still life from
different positions, followed by a partner comparison. Editing covers crop,
straighten and brightness. No paid app, public upload or photograph of another
person is required.

Participant profiles constrain attendance, seat, phone, direct help, submissions
and knowledge. Do not fill a conversation with the whole event history. Someone
who leaves before critique cannot assess it firsthand; a G02 member cannot claim
their own light practice lost ten minutes to the second-rotation rain. Hearsay
must remain hearsay. A person may notice an issue without minding it, or praise
one exercise and criticize another. Authoring IDs must not appear in bot prompts
or participant speech.

## Proposed short interview for fixtures

The neutral opening is: «Γεια σου! Πώς σου φάνηκε συνολικά το σημερινό εργαστήριο
φωτογραφίας με κινητό; Αν θέλεις, πες μας με λίγα λόγια.»

Allow **at most one useful, short, open clarification**, based only on what the
person says. For an otherwise unclear «μας τρέξανε λίγο», an appropriate question
is «Σε ποιο μέρος του εργαστηρίου αναφέρεσαι;» Do not supply an exercise, rain,
sound issue or criticism for the participant to endorse. If their answer already
provides useful context, thank them without another question. Do not require a
rating or exhaust a list of topics.

Respect refusal and STOP immediately: a brief acknowledgment, no further question
or persuasion. Silence contributes no participant testimony. An unanswered
follow-up stays partial. These authored bot turns describe intended fixture
behavior, not an observed application run or a new production questionnaire.

## Useful difficult cases

- «Ήταν λίγο λίγο» without clarification: preserve uncertainty about the target.
- Praise for learning exposure plus criticism of light-practice time in one reply.
- A correction from «έξω» to «στην επεξεργασία»: use the corrected exercise.
- Sarcastic «τέλεια, πρόλαβα μία φωτογραφία» from a rain-affected participant.
- A G02 participant repeats a G09 friend's complaint as hearsay.
- A right-rear listener reports screen and sound problems together.
- An advanced attendee dislikes editing while a beginner values the same segment.
- A person asks about the handout before it is due: do not infer broken delivery.
- A participant declines after mentioning a problem; do not pursue clarification.
- A silent invitee or abandoned follow-up: no invented answer or completion.
